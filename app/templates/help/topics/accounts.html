An account is one advertiser: a parish, school, business, or person who buys ads or receives mailings. Each account has a six-character account number, such as 004017.

Finding an account

The Accounts page lists every advertiser. Use the filters to narrow it:

  • Search finds text in the name, account number, contact, city, e-mail, phone, or category.
  • Show limits the list to current advertisers, past advertisers, prospects, or the e-mail list.
  • Sort by orders the list by name, by account number (newest first), or by the most recent ad.

The list also shows each account's number of ads and the date of its last ad.

Creating an account

  1. Choose New account on the Accounts page. This requires the Editor or Admin role.
  2. The system suggests the next free account number. Keep it unless you have a reason to use another. An account number uses letters and digits only, up to six characters.
  3. Enter at least the Advertiser name, plus any contact details, and choose Create account.

Before you create an account, search for the advertiser first, so that the same advertiser does not end up with two accounts.

Viewing and editing an account

Choose an account to open it. The heading shows the advertiser's name in bold, with the account number below it (for example, Account: 002015). The page has four parts:

  • Contact: name, address, phone, e-mail, web site, and Directory Years.
  • Billing Address: where invoices go, when that differs from the contact address. Leave it blank when bills go to the contact address.
  • Client Information: category, special advertising opportunities, notes, and the status boxes.
  • Advertising History: every ad for this account, newest first, with the total. Choose Add ad here to add one.

Editors can change any field and choose Save. The account number appears only in the heading and cannot change, because other records and documents outside this system refer to it. Viewers see the same page in read-only form.

Use Previous and Next to move between accounts in account-number order, as in Access.

Status boxes

Current AdvertiserAdvertises now.
Past AdvertiserHas advertised before but not currently.
ProspectHas not advertised yet; a sales lead.
E-mail ListReceives e-mail about advertising opportunities.

The boxes are independent: an account can have any combination. Search and the advertiser lists use them as filters.

Billing address

Mailing labels and address lists always use the contact address. The billing address is kept on the account for invoicing.

Directory Years

List the years the advertiser appears in the directory, separated by commas or spaces, for example 2025, 2026. The mailing labels by directory year report reads this field.

Deleting an account

Only Admins can delete an account, and only when it has no ads. If the account has ads, move or delete them first. Usually it is better to clear the Current Advertiser box than to delete the account, because the history then stays available.
