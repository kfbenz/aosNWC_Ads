The audit log records every change to accounts, ads, the lookup lists, and the normalization map: who made it, when, and what changed.

Reading the log

Each row shows:

When (UTC)The time of the change in Coordinated Universal Time. Seattle is 8 hours behind UTC in winter and 7 hours behind in summer.
UserThe person who made the change, by sign-in name.
Actioninsert (created), update (changed), or delete (deleted).
RecordThe table and the record's key: an account number, or an ad number.
ChangesFor an update, each changed field with its old and new value. For a delete, a copy of the deleted record.

Finding a change

  • Table: choose accounts or ad_placements (ads), or one of the lookup tables.
  • Record ID: an account number, such as 004017, or an ad number, such as 11872.
  • User: part of a person's sign-in name.

Combine the filters to answer questions such as "who changed the address on account 004017?".

Restoring a value

The log does not restore anything by itself. To put back an earlier value, find the change in the log, open the record, and enter the old value again. A deleted ad or account must be re-created by hand from the copy in the log.
