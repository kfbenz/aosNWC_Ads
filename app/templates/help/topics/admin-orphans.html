An orphan ad has an account number that does not match any account. Most came from Access, where an ad could be saved under a number typed slightly differently, such as 755 instead of 000755, or under an account that was later deleted. Orphan ads do not appear in an account's history, in Search by account details, or on advertiser address lists.

The Orphan ads page

Admin › Orphan ads groups the orphan ads by their old account number. Each group shows the ads, their run dates, and their total. Use Find to look for an old number, an ad description, or text in the notes.

Attaching ads to an existing account

  1. Check the ads in the group, and open one if needed, to identify the advertiser.
  2. The Attach to account box may already hold a suggestion. The system suggests an account when the old number differs only by leading zeros or capitals, and shows that account's name. Check that the name is right.
  3. Otherwise, search for the account in the same box. Type part of the advertiser's name, the account number, the contact's name, or the city, for example Mary or Tacoma. The box lists every matching account as number - name - contact - city. Choose the right one. You can also type just the account number.
  4. Choose Attach. The system attaches every ad in the group.

Creating the missing account

If the advertiser has no account, enter the advertiser's name next to or create account ... named and choose Create and attach. The system creates the account with the old number and attaches the ads. Then open the account and fill in its address and other details.

This option appears only when the old number is a valid account number: letters and digits only, up to six characters.

Leaving a group for later

Do nothing with a group you cannot identify yet. It stays on the page until someone resolves it.

What the system keeps

  • Each ad keeps its original Access account number, so the change can always be traced.
  • The audit log records each attached ad and each created account.
