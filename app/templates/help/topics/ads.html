An ad is one placement in one issue: what ran, when it was sold and run, its price, and who sold it. Every ad belongs to an account.

The Ads list

The Ads page lists every ad, newest run date first, 50 to a page. Use the filters to narrow it:

  • Search finds text in the advertiser name, the ad description, the account number, or the bill number.
  • Sales rep shows one rep's ads. Choose (none) for ads without a rep.
  • Run date from / to limits the ads to a range of issues.

The count and total amount above the list cover every ad that matches, not only the page you see.

For more detailed questions, such as "current advertisers in Seattle whose ads ran last December", use Search or Advertiser lists.

Viewing an ad

Choose an ad to see all its fields and its advertiser. Editors also see an Edit ad button.

Adding an ad

  1. Open the advertiser under Accounts.
  2. Choose Add ad in the Advertising History section.
  3. The form starts with the Comm %, Sales, and Auth By of the account's most recent ad, and today's date as Date Sold. Change them as needed.
  4. Fill in the other fields and choose Add ad.

Adding an ad requires the Editor or Admin role.

Editing an ad

Choose Edit next to an ad in an account's Advertising History, or Edit ad on the ad's own page. Change the fields and choose Save. The fields are:

FieldWhat to enter
Date SoldThe date the advertiser bought the ad. The commission report groups ads by this date.
Date RunThe issue date the ad appears in. The publication report and exports use this date.
AmountThe price in dollars, for example 450 or 450.00. A credit can be negative.
Comm %The sales commission as a percentage: enter 10 for 10%, not 0.10. Leave it blank or enter 0 when no commission applies.
SalesThe sales rep's code, for example KH.
Auth ByThe person at the advertiser who approved the ad.
SizeThe ad size, for example 1/4 page.
SectionThe section or special issue, for example a Christmas or graduation section.
PageThe page the ad appears on.
Run #The run code, as used in Access.
Bill #The invoice number.
DescA short description of the ad.
NotesAnything else worth keeping.

Sales, Size, and Section

When an Admin has set up the list of allowed values for one of these fields, the field becomes a dropdown, and you must choose a value from it. If you need a value that is not on the list, ask an Admin to add it.

An older ad may show a value marked (legacy, not on the list). That ad keeps its value, and you can still save it. Choose a value from the list to replace it.

When a field has no list yet, it accepts any text and suggests values that other ads already use.

Deleting an ad

Only Admins can delete an ad, with the Delete ad button at the bottom of its edit page. Deleting cannot be undone, although the audit log keeps a copy of the deleted ad.
