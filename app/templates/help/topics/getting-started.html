NWC Ads keeps the advertising records for Northwest Catholic: advertisers (accounts), the ads they buy, and the reports staff use for commission, publication, and mailings. It replaces the Access database.

Signing in

Open the site and sign in with your Archdiocese Microsoft account, the same one you use for Outlook and Teams. You stay signed in for up to eight hours. Choose Sign out at the top right when you finish on a shared computer.

If you see No access yet after signing in, your account does not have a role in NWC Ads. Ask IT to assign you one of the roles below.

Roles

Your role appears next to your name at the top right. It controls what you can do:

RoleCan do
ViewerView ads and accounts, search, and run every report, export, and label sheet.
EditorEverything a Viewer can do, plus create and edit accounts and add and edit ads.
AdminEverything an Editor can do, plus delete ads and accounts, manage lookup lists, merge spellings, fix orphan ads, and read the audit log.

A page that your role cannot use shows Not allowed. Buttons that your role cannot use do not appear.

The menu

  • Accounts: the advertisers. Open one to see its details and full advertising history, and to add an ad.
  • Ads: every ad, newest run date first, with filters.
  • Search: the Access advanced search, across both advertisers and ads.
  • Reports: commission, publication, mailing labels, advertiser lists, and Excel exports.
  • Admin (Admins only): lookup lists, merge spellings, orphan ads, and the audit log.
  • Help: this guide.

Saving, and edits by two people at once

Changes take effect when you choose Save. If you leave a page without saving, the system discards your changes.

If someone else saves the same account or ad while you are editing it, the system does not overwrite their work. Instead, it shows the message "Someone else saved this ... while you were editing it" and reloads the record with their changes. Enter your changes again and save.

The system records every change, with the person's name and the time. An account or ad shows "Last changed by" next to its Save button.

Printing

The site header, menu, and footer do not print. Reports have a Print / Save as PDF button. For mailing labels, see Mailing labels.
