Terms

Account number
The advertiser's six-character number, such as 004017. It never changes once the account exists.
Advertiser
The name of the account: a parish, school, business, organization, or person.
Date Sold
The date the advertiser bought the ad. The commission report uses it.
Date Run
The date of the issue in which the ad appears. The publication report, advertiser lists, and exports use it.
Comm %
The sales commission percentage on an ad. Enter 10 for 10%. Commission earned is the amount multiplied by this percentage.
Directory Years
The years in which the advertiser appears in the directory. The mailing labels report reads this field.
Status boxes
Current Advertiser, Past Advertiser, Prospect, and E-mail List on each account. They are independent of each other.
Lookup list
The allowed values for an ad's Sales, Size, or Section field. Admins maintain them.
Legacy value
A value on an older ad that is not on the lookup list. The ad keeps it until someone chooses a value from the list.
Orphan ad
An ad whose account number does not match any account, usually because of a typing difference in Access. Admins fix these.
Audit log
The record of every change: who made it, when, and the old and new values.

Common questions

I cannot see a Save button or an Add ad button.

Your role is Viewer, which is read-only. If your work requires changes, ask IT for the Editor role.

The site says "No access yet".

You signed in, but your account has no NWC Ads role. Ask IT to assign one.

The site says "Someone else saved this ... while you were editing it".

Another person saved the same record first. The page now shows their version. Enter your changes again and save.

The Sales, Size, or Section dropdown does not have the value I need.

Ask an Admin to add it to the lookup list. Until then, an older ad can keep its current value.

I entered 0.10 in Comm % and the ad shows 0.1%.

Comm % takes a percentage. Edit the ad and enter 10 for 10%.

My labels do not line up with the stickers.

Print at Actual size (100%), not "Fit to page", and use Avery 5160 or 8160 sheets. See Printing labels.

A search finds fewer accounts than I expected.

In AND mode, every field must match, and ad fields must match the same ad. Switch to OR, or fill in fewer fields. Also check the status boxes, which always narrow the results.

I made a mistake. Can it be undone?

Edit the record again and correct it. If you deleted something by mistake, or cannot tell what changed, ask an Admin: the audit log shows the earlier values.
